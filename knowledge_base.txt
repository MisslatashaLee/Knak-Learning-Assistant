Building Emails
Email Basics
What is an email template?
An email template is a reusable framework that contains predefined branding, layouts, colors, fonts, and content areas. Templates help organizations maintain consistency across multiple email campaigns.
What is an email fragment?
An email fragment is a reusable section of content that can be inserted into multiple emails. Common examples include disclaimers, footers, banners, promotional messages, and call-to-action sections.
What is an email module?
An email module is an individual content block within an email. Examples include text sections, image blocks, buttons, video placeholders, product information sections, and social media links.
What is a reusable component?
A reusable component is a content element that can be used repeatedly across multiple communications. Reusable components improve efficiency, reduce duplicate work, and help ensure consistency.
What is responsive email design?
Responsive email design ensures that an email automatically adapts to different screen sizes and devices, providing a consistent experience on desktops, tablets, and smartphones.

Image quality
Working links
Button destinations
Mobile responsiveness
Personalization fields
Accessibility requirements
Approval status
Brand consistency
How do I review an email for quality?
Quality review includes checking content accuracy, grammar, layout consistency, image rendering, link functionality, personalization fields, accessibility requirements, and overall user experience.
Why should I test an email before sending it?
Testing helps identify issues before recipients receive the email. It can reveal broken links, formatting problems, missing images, personalization errors, and inconsistent rendering across devices.
What is email rendering?
Email rendering refers to how an email displays across different devices, email providers, operating systems, and screen sizes. Different email clients may display content differently.
What are common email design mistakes?
Common mistakes include:
Too much text
Poor mobile experience
Broken links
Missing alt text
Inconsistent branding
Large image files
Too many call-to-action buttons
Poor readability

Landing Pages
What is a landing page?
A landing page is a standalone web page designed to encourage visitors to take a specific action, such as registering for an event, downloading a document, or submitting a form.
How is a landing page different from an email?
An email is delivered directly to a recipient's inbox, while a landing page is a webpage accessed through a link. Emails often drive traffic to landing pages where users complete an action.
What makes a good landing page?
A good landing page includes:
Clear headline
Focused message
Strong call-to-action
Relevant imagery
Easy navigation
Mobile responsiveness
Fast page loading
When should I use a landing page?
Landing pages are useful when collecting registrations, promoting products, capturing leads, providing detailed information, or supporting marketing campaigns.

Personalization
Personalization
What is personalization?
Personalization is the process of tailoring content to individual recipients based on available information such as name, location, preferences, or customer profile data.
What is a merge tag?
A merge tag is a placeholder that is automatically replaced with personalized information when the email is sent. For example, a merge tag may insert a recipient's first name into an email.
Why are merge tags used?
Merge tags help create a more personalized experience by automatically adding recipient-specific information without requiring manual updates.
What should be checked before using personalization?
Before using personalization:
Verify data accuracy
Confirm merge tags are configured correctly
Test sample records
Validate fallback values
Review personalization rules

Reporting
How do you measure email success?
Email success is commonly measured using metrics such as opens, clicks, click-through rates, conversions, engagement levels, and overall campaign performance against objectives.
What is an email open rate?
Open rate is the percentage of recipients who open an email compared to the total number of delivered emails.
What is a click-through rate?
Click-through rate (CTR) is the percentage of recipients who click on one or more links within an email after receiving it.
Why is email reporting important?
Email reporting helps organizations understand audience engagement, identify opportunities for improvement, measure campaign performance, and make data-driven marketing decisions.

Q: What is HTML?
Answer:
HTML (HyperText Markup Language) is the code used to structure and display content on websites, emails, and landing pages. Traditionally, developers manually write HTML code, but modern content authoring tools can generate HTML automatically.

Answer:
Automated HTML generation allows marketers to focus on creating content rather than coding. It can reduce production time, improve consistency, minimize coding errors, and make it easier to update content without technical expertise.
